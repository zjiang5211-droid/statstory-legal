StatStory — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 22, 2026

StatStory is a data-visualization content service that produces short videos ("one counterintuitive number, visualized, every day") and publishes them to social media platforms, including TikTok, through official platform APIs.

Use of the Service

The StatStory application connects only to accounts owned and authorized by StatStory. It is used solely to upload and publish StatStory's own original video content, and to read basic account and content statistics.

Authorization

Account owners authorize the application via the platform's official OAuth flow. Authorization can be revoked at any time in the platform's account settings, after which the application can no longer access the account.

Content

All videos published through the application are original works created from public data sources (e.g. Our World in Data, Wikipedia, official statistics), with sources credited in the content.
