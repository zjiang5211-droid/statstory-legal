StatStory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

StatStory is a data-visualization content service that publishes its own short videos to social media platforms, including TikTok, through official platform APIs.

Data We Process

The StatStory application processes only the data required to operate its own accounts: OAuth access tokens issued by the platform, basic profile information of the authorized StatStory account (username, account ID), and statistics of StatStory's own published content.

What We Do Not Do

We do not collect personal data from TikTok users or viewers. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing. We do not track users across apps or websites.

Storage and Deletion

Access tokens are stored securely and are deleted when authorization is revoked or the integration is discontinued. Account owners may request deletion of any stored data at any time via the contact below.
